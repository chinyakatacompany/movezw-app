MoveZW — Move Anything, Anywhere in Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe's trusted transport marketplace. Post a transport request, compare offers from ID-verified drivers, and track your delivery in real time — from Harare to Bulawayo and beyond. Free to join; you only pay the agreed delivery price to your driver.

How it works

  1. Post your transport request — tell us what you're moving, where it's going, and your budget.
  2. Receive offers from verified drivers — background-checked transporters send competitive quotes.
  3. Choose the best offer and track delivery in real time until it arrives.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book transport on MoveZW?

Create a free account, tap "Book Transport", enter your pickup, destination, cargo details and budget, then post your request. Verified drivers will send you offers to compare.

Are the drivers verified?

Yes. Every driver submits their National ID, driver's licence and vehicle registration, which our admin team reviews before approval.

How do I pay for my delivery?

MoveZW currently supports cash on delivery.

Can I track my delivery?

Yes — once you accept an offer, you can follow your delivery in real time from pickup through to final delivery.

How do I become a driver?

Sign up as a driver, complete your profile, and upload your documents. After admin verification you'll start receiving transport requests in your area.

Is there a fee to use MoveZW?

Creating an account and posting transport requests is free. You only pay the agreed delivery price to your chosen driver.
